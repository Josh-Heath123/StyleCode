# Veldt Veterinary Clinics: online booking

Veldt Veterinary Clinics, a made-up chain of three vet clinics in Gauteng (Centurion, Irene and Olievenhoutbosch), wants online booking and reminders to replace phone bookings and a paper diary at each clinic. The meeting covers their practice software (VetDesk Pro), POPIA and owners' phone numbers, the vaccination-season rush, the budget and a February deadline, with a few questions left open.

## Who is who

- **Nomvula Khumalo:** the consultant (consultant's side).
- **Hennie Swanepoel:** vet and owner, Veldt Veterinary Clinics (client).
- **Charmaine Pillay:** practice manager, Veldt Veterinary Clinics (client).

## Consent

This is a role-played meeting written for testing. There are no real people, companies or recordings in it. In the story, Nomvula asks at the start (T1), and both client participants agree to the transcript and to the AI on her own computer (T2 and T3).

## The file

`zoom-saved-transcript.txt` is typed as a Zoom "Save transcript" `.txt`: a `[Name] HH:MM:SS` line (time of day, starting 14:00:05), the words, then a blank line, with Windows (CRLF) line endings. It has 59 turns (T1 to T59, one per speaker block, in file order).

`answer-key.draft.json` is a draft answer key (25 findings), written with the meeting by a subagent (Claude). It has not been checked by the user yet.
