# Umoya College: parent portal

Umoya College, a made-up independent school with about 900 learners, wants a parent portal for fee statements, reports, notices and absence notes, fed from its old school-admin system (SchoolBase 7). The meeting covers parents' mobile data costs, the security of learners' records, the report-day peak, language and accessibility, and a budget that still needs board approval, with risks, open questions and a decision on a trial.

## Who is who

- **Priya Govender:** the consultant (consultant's side).
- **Lindiwe Zulu:** bursar, Umoya College (client).
- **Graham Fortuin:** deputy principal, Umoya College (client).
- **Ayanda Ngcobo:** part-time IT administrator, Umoya College (client).

## Consent

This is a role-played meeting written for testing. There are no real people, companies or recordings in it. In the story, Priya asks at the start (T1), and all three client participants agree to the transcript and to the AI on her own computer (T2 to T4).

## The file

`google-meet-transcript.txt` is typed as a Google Meet transcript downloaded as plain text: a UTF-8 byte-order mark, the title line `... (2026-11-09 15:30 GMT+2) - Transcript`, `Attendees` and the list, `Transcript`, time lines (`00:00:00`, `00:05:02`, `00:10:04`), `Name: words` paragraphs, `Meeting ended after 00:11:56 👋` and the disclaimer. Line endings are `\n`, as in the Karoo Freight sample. It has 59 turns (T1 to T59, one per speaker paragraph, in file order).

`answer-key.draft.json` is a draft answer key (25 findings), written with the meeting by a subagent (Claude). It has not been checked by the user yet.
